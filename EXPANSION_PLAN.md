# Peek-a-Keep campaign direction

Version 1.5 folds the replay-mode ideas into one 19-siege journey. The player continues the campaign instead of choosing daily, endless, challenge or terrain modes.

Terrain is part of the authored pacing: teach the bridge queue, then winding mountain trajectories, then alternating courtyard approaches before combining them with mortars and repair wagons. Keep the hold/release control unchanged.

The former challenge restrictions become late outposts with an explained reason: no workshop at the old mountain keep, two hearts at a wounded courtyard, and a four-ram bridge convoy. The daily battery becomes a stable mixed muster, so retries teach the same timing. Endless escalation becomes a finite three-wave long watch, with field-clear recovery rather than another upgrade menu. An escorted fortress closes the campaign.

Existing siege numbers and saves remain useful. Tester access and a visible reset control support direct feedback on every defense. See [README.md](README.md) for their operation and exact campaign rules.

Future additions should deepen this journey with authored enemy interactions and better readable trajectories. Version 1.7 adds authored rain, fog and dusk; the star-funded workshop now activates every rank immediately, including advanced upgrades with increasing small-branch requirements of 3/5/7/9/10. Keep required grinding, separate modes and multiplayer deferred.
